Our story

Why we started

◎
The problem we kept seeing
Too much e-learning is built for compliance boxes, not for learners. Templates get recycled, content gets dumped on teams, and nothing changes. We saw good organisations spending real money on training no one actually wanted to do.
✏
What we do differently
We start with the learners. Every project begins with a proper needs analysis, not a template. We use the pedagogical principles we've developed over 15+ years in education, and we build content that stands up in both academic and corporate environments.
How we work

Our principles

Four things that shape every project we take on.

1
Pedagogy first, tools second
Storyline, Rise, Learnworlds — they're just delivery. What matters is whether the learning actually sticks. We design for the brain, then choose the platform.
2
Bespoke, always
No templates with your logo slapped on. Every course, every toolkit, every LMS is built from scratch around your learners, your goals and your context.
3
We stay after launch
The real work starts when the course goes live. We support deployment, gather feedback and stay involved as long as you need us.
4
Plain speaking
No jargon, no fluff, no consultant theatre. We tell you what will work, what won't, and what we think you should do next. Even when it's uncomfortable.
