Cookie Policy.

What cookies and similar technologies we use on this site, why we use them, and how to control them.

Last updated: 23 April 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use on exceleratelearning.co.uk, and how you can control them. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy, which describes how we handle personal data more broadly.

This policy is written to comply with the UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

1.What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit it. They let the site remember you, how you've used it, and what your preferences are. Some cookies are set by the site you're visiting (first-party cookies); others are set by third parties whose services the site uses (third-party cookies).

This policy also covers similar technologies — localStorage, pixels, tags — that work in the same way as cookies.

2.How we use cookies

We try to keep cookie use on this site to a minimum. We use them to:

  • Make the site work properly and remember your choices (for example, whether you've accepted or declined analytics cookies).
  • Understand how visitors use the site in aggregate, so we can improve it.

We do not use cookies for advertising, retargeting, or cross-site tracking.

Categories explained:

  • Essential — needed for the site to work or to remember your consent choice. These are set without requiring consent.
  • Optional — analytics — help us understand site usage. These are only set if you accept.
  • Anonymous — no cookies — measurement that doesn't use cookies or persistent identifiers, so doesn't require consent under PECR.
  • Third-party (on click) — set by an external service only when you choose to engage with it (for example, booking via Calendly).

4.Your choices

When you first visit the site, you'll see a cookie banner asking whether you accept optional analytics cookies. You can:

  • Accept all — we'll set essential and analytics cookies.
  • Reject optional — we'll only set essential cookies. Analytics will be disabled.
  • Change your mind any time — click Cookie settings in the footer of any page to revisit the banner.

You can also manage or delete cookies directly in your browser. The ICO's guide to cookies is a good starting point. Most browsers also let you block third-party cookies or browse in a private/incognito window that clears them at the end of the session.

5.Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties we use to run the site (Google, Cloudflare, Calendly). We've linked to each provider's own privacy information below so you can read how they process data:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Calendly Privacy Notice

6.Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes to the cookies we use or to comply with legal requirements. The "last updated" date at the top of the page shows when the most recent change was made. If we add new optional cookies, we'll ask for your consent again before setting them.
